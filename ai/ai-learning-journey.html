Portfolio Artifact | AI Learning Architecture

From AI Awareness to Workflow Adoption

This portfolio-safe framework demonstrates how I architect enterprise AI learning. It is intentionally generalized and does not reproduce proprietary employer content.

01 · ORIENT

Build shared AI fluency

Establish practical foundations: what the tools can do, where they fail, responsible-use expectations, and how to recognize valuable use cases.

02 · PRACTICE

Learn through real work

Move from generic prompting to role-relevant scenarios, reusable prompt patterns, demonstrations, and guided practice tied to actual workflows.

03 · APPLY

Embed new behaviors

Support employees with in-flow resources, champions, manager reinforcement, office hours, and targeted content at moments of need.

04 · SCALE

Measure and iterate

Use adoption, confidence, application, feedback, and business signals to find friction, improve pathways, and scale what works.

Design principles

Role relevance over generic AI literacy. Practice over passive consumption. Reusable systems over one-time courses. Responsible experimentation over fear. Business outcomes over completion metrics.
